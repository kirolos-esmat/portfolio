Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scrow Calculator ("we," "us," or "our"), a score tracking application for multiplayer games. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services. By using Scrow Calculator, you agree to this Privacy Policy, which outlines our practices regarding the handling of your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Game Data: Player names, number of players, round configurations, scores, and game history that you input into the application.
  • Local Storage: All game data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Technical Data: Basic information about your device and app usage to improve functionality and fix bugs.
  • Preferences: Your language preference (Arabic or English) is stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Score Tracking: To store and display game scores, player names, and game history locally on your device.
  • App Functionality: To provide features such as score calculation, multipliers, round management, and game history.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our application's user interface and functionality.
  • Preferences: To remember your language preference and other app settings.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Local Storage Only: All game data, including player names, scores, and history, is stored locally on your device and is not shared with our servers or any third parties.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not use cloud services to store your personal game data.
  • Analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the app, but this does not include your game data or personal information.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All your game data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by your operating system.
  • Data Control: You have full control over your data. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device.
  • No Cloud Backup: Your game data is not backed up to cloud services, so we recommend taking your own backups if you want to preserve your game history.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information:

  • Access and view all your game data within the app
  • Edit or delete individual games from your history
  • Clear all data by uninstalling the app or using app settings
  • Control your data without needing to contact us

If you have questions about your data or privacy, please contact us at kirolos.esmat10@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Scrow Calculator is suitable for users of all ages. Since all data is stored locally on the device and we do not collect personal information from our servers, we do not knowingly collect data from children. Parents or guardians can monitor their child's use of the app and delete any data directly from the device.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes through the app or by updating this page. Your continued use of Scrow Calculator after such modifications constitutes your acknowledgment of the modified Privacy Policy.
